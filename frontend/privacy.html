nightcord is a late-night study space for college students, run by [OPERATOR NAME] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details — your school email address and name or display name. Sign-up, sign-in and passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Clerk; we never see or store your password. If you choose “Continue with Google” or “Microsoft”, that provider shares your name and email with us.
  • School and timezone — worked out from your email’s domain, so study rooms open at night where your school is. If your school’s timezone isn’t known, we use the one your browser reports.
  • What you post — rooms you create and the messages you send. These are visible to other signed-in students during night hours.
  • Technical information — your IP address, used briefly to limit abuse (rate limiting) and not stored; and basic server logs (which page or endpoint was requested and when — never message contents or passwords).
  • Crash reports — only if you turn on Diagnostics (see Cookies below): technical details of an error, without your name or email.
  • Your cookie choices — once you’re signed in, we keep a record of the choices you make and when, as proof of your consent.

Why we use it

  • To run nightcord for you: your account, the night-hours schedule for your school, rooms and chat (necessary to provide the service).
  • To keep it safe: checking you’re a college student, stopping bots and spam, and investigating abuse (our legitimate interest in a safe service).
  • Optional extras, only with your consent: remembering your theme, and crash reports.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.
